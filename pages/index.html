ACOUSTIC BLUEPRINT & TOLERANCES

Anatomy of an Engineered Transducer

Every milligram of moving mass and micron of clearance is optimized for extreme magnetic flux utilization, symmetrical restoring force, and zero thermal delamination.

[NODE 01] TOLERANCE ±0.05 MM

Voice Coil Magnetic Gap Dynamics

Precision internal diameter tolerances of ±0.05 mm guarantee optimal flux utilization in narrow magnetic air gaps without coil rubbing, even under high-temperature expansion and high excursion.

[NODE 02] SYMMETRIC Kms(x)

Suspension Linearization & Compliance

Nomex spider wave geometry is calibrated in tandem with progressive roll surround height to maintain linear restoring compliance, suppressing odd-order harmonic distortion at peak excursion.

[NODE 03] HIGH-Q MATRIX

Diaphragm Stiff-to-Mass Ratio

Virgin unbleached softwood fibers blended with water-resistant acoustic coatings yield a high modulus-of-elasticity cone body, pushing breakup resonances well outside the passband.

[NODE 04] 280°C CONTINUOUS

Polyimide Thermal Dissipation

Space-grade polymer adhesives baked in programmed curing ovens withstand continuous 280°C thermal spikes without bond breakdown, wire unspooling, or former separation.

[NODE 05] STITCHED TINSEL

Fatigue-Resistant Lead Wire Weaves

Heavy-gauge copper-silver tinsel braided around aramid fibers stitched directly through spiders eliminates lead slap against the cone and prevents premature fatigue fracture under heavy bass load.

MANUFACTURING WORKFLOW

Precision Four-Stage Production Flow

From raw fiber suspension to CNC winding and micrometer laser inspection at our Pratap Nagar, Jaipur facility.

01

Pulp Slurry & Vacuum Tooling

Long-fiber unbleached softwood pulp is blended with proprietary water-resistant polymers and vacuum-drawn onto acoustic cone tooling for consistent wall density and zero internal voids.

02

CNC Precision Coil Winding

High-tension CNC winding mandrels apply edge-wound flat wire or round copper with micron-level pitch accuracy and computer-metered aerospace polyimide resin.

03

Oven Polymerization & Curing

Assembled voice coils undergo 4-hour programmed thermal curing cycles up to 280°C, permanently baking adhesives into indestructible structural bonds resistant to extreme voice coil heat.

04

Laser Micrometer & Acoustic QC

100% optical inspection of inner diameters, winding heights, DC resistance (Rdc), and physical mass tolerances before vacuum packaging in anti-static shipping trays.
