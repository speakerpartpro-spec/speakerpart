OEM Loudspeaker Component Manufacturing Process

From raw cellulose pulp and aerospace polyimide formers to high-excursion surrounds and dynamic transducer sub-assemblies, explore our strict six-stage manufacturing methodology.

01

Raw Material Verification & Ingot Inspection

All incoming materials undergo strict physical and chemical verification. High-purity oxygen-free copper and edge-wound flat CCAW (Copper-Clad Aluminum Wire) are inspected for uniform resistivity and dielectric coating thickness. Former substrates (Kapton, TIL glass composite, titanium) undergo tensile yield testing and surface degreasing to guarantee chemical bonding with high-temperature epoxy resins.

02

Precision Voice Coil CNC Winding & Thermal Baking

Coils are wound on multi-axis CNC micro-tension winding machines to prevent wire stretching or micro-fractures. Topologies include 2-layer, 4-layer, 8-layer, and inside/outside (in-out) dual winding for high-power touring drivers. Wounds are saturated with aerospace-grade thermosetting resin and cured in multi-stage programmable thermal convection ovens at 250°C to 280°C for permanent structural lock.

03

Non-Pressed Pulp Cone Molding & Air-Drying

Cones are formed from custom virgin kraft cellulose pulp blended with carbon fiber, Kevlar, and bamboo fibers in an aqueous slurry. Vacuum-assisted mesh molds shape the conical geometry without mechanical pressing, preserving fiber porosity for high internal damping. The cones are air-dried in temperature-controlled chambers and coated with tropicalized PVA and butyl damping agents to resist humid environments.

04

Nomex / Conex Spider Pressing & Lead Wire Stitching

Spiders are stamped from resin-impregnated Nomex/Conex woven fabrics using precision CNC heated dies. Progressive roll profiles are calibrated for linear mechanical compliance over long excursion travels. For SPL subwoofers, heavy braided silver/copper tinsel lead wires are stitched directly into the spider rolls using automated multi-point stitching to eliminate lead slap.

05

Thermoformed Polyether Surround Bonding & Stitching

UV-stabilized high-density polyether foam is compression-thermoformed into wide-roll (EROM) geometries. Surrounds are bonded to cones using flexible high-shear cyanoacrylate and rubberized T-7000 adhesives. For high-output woofers, cones and surrounds are reinforced with double/triple pass heavy nylon perimeter stitching to prevent separation under extreme acoustic pressure.

06

100% Dimensional & Multi-Frequency QC Verification

Every production batch is verified against tight engineering tolerances: micrometer voice coil internal diameter (±0.05 mm), mass balancing (±1-2 g on cones), DCR resistance (±0.2 ohm), and continuous resonant frequency (Fs) sweep tests. Completed components are packaged in moisture-barrier bags and reinforced export master cartons.
