Terms of Supply & Export Commercial Policies

Commercial Export Provisions • Brand: SPEAKERPARTPRO • Master Acoustic Engineering

These Terms of Supply govern all wholesale orders, evaluation sample shipments, OEM tooling agreements, and commercial export contracts executed by SPEAKERPARTPRO.

1. Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)

- Evaluation Samples: Pre-production evaluation orders are available in quantities of 5 to 50 units per SKU.
- Standard Production Run: 500 units per model.
- Custom OEM Tooling: Custom voice coil heights or custom paper cone molds require a minimum volume of 1,000 to 2,500 units depending on geometry.

2. International Incoterms & Freight

All shipments are dispatched under standard ICC Incoterms 2020:

  • Air Courier: DAP (Delivered at Place) or DDU (Delivered Duty Unpaid) via DHL Express or FedEx Priority.
  • Ocean Freight: FOB Nhava Sheva (JNPT, Mumbai), CFR, or CIF destination seaport.
  • Customs Tariff: All loudspeaker components are declared under Indian HS Code 8518.90.00.

3. Quality Tolerances & Warranty

All parts are manufactured within strict electro-acoustic tolerances: - Voice Coil Inner Diameter: ±0.05 mm
- Voice Coil DCR Resistance: ±0.2 Ω
- Paper Cone Mass Tolerance: ±1.0 g to ±2.0 g
Defective items reported with photographic verification within 14 days of receipt are replaced in subsequent production batches or credited on account.

4. Payment Terms

Evaluation sample orders are settled via International Wire Transfer (SWIFT T/T) or verified digital gateway. Production orders require 50% advance deposit with the remaining 50% due against Bill of Lading (B/L) copy or prior to DHL dispatch.
