Privacy Policy

Brand: SPEAKERPARTPRO • Precision Transducer Systems • Last Updated: September 2026

SPEAKERPARTPRO is committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring the security of all commercial and personal information collected through our website and B2B inquiry desks.

1. Information We Collect

We collect business contact information (name, company name, corporate email address, WhatsApp/phone numbers, delivery destination, and component engineering requirements) submitted through our quotation request forms and inquiry desks to formulate B2B quotations, process sample orders, and fulfill export shipments.

2. Use of Information

Information collected is strictly used for commercial trade operations:

  • Generating proforma invoices, commercial invoices, and export packing lists.
  • Arranging international air express (DHL/FedEx) or ocean freight booking.
  • Providing technical consultations regarding electro-acoustic parts.
  • Fulfilling Indian Customs and overseas import declarations under HS Code 8518.90.00.

3. Non-Disclosure of Proprietary Designs

All proprietary acoustic CAD drawings, custom voice coil winding topologies, and dimensional specifications shared with SPEAKERPARTPRO are held under strict non-disclosure. We do not distribute or cross-sell client-commissioned OEM parts to third parties without written authorization.
